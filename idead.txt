## 💰 Subscription and Upload Limit Logic

You can create a dual-sided revenue model where both the **Certificate Uploader** (the professional) and the **Certificate Viewer** (e.g., a recruiter or peer) subscribe for enhanced features.

---

### 1. Uploader (Professional) Tiers

This model dictates the privileges for the person **uploading and sharing** their certificates.

| Feature | **Free Tier (Basic)** | **Pro Tier (Standard Subscription)** | **Business Tier (Premium Subscription)** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Certificate Upload Limit** | 5 Certificates Max | **Unlimited Uploads** | Unlimited Uploads |
| **Profile & Branding** | Basic Profile URL, Platform Logo/Branding present. | Basic Profile URL, **Option to remove Platform Branding.** | **Custom Subdomain URL** (e.g., `john.yourplatform.com`), No Branding. |
| **Analytics** | Basic View Count (last 30 days). | **Advanced Analytics** (Geo-location of viewers, Time spent, Top viewed). | All Pro Analytics + **PDF Export of Portfolio Performance.** |
| **Verification** | Self-Verification (untrusted). | Option to submit for **Manual Trust Score Review**. | **Priority Manual Review** and **"Verified Uploader" Badge.** |
| **Support** | Standard Email Support. | Priority Email/Chat Support. | Dedicated Account Manager. |

---

### 2. Viewer (Recruiter/Peer) Tiers

This model is typically aimed at organizations (recruiters, academic institutions) or very engaged peers who need to view and assess many profiles.

| Feature | **Guest Viewer (Free)** | **Recruiter Tier (Subscription)** | **API Access Tier (Enterprise)** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Profile Viewing** | Unlimited public profile views. | Unlimited public profile views. | Unlimited public profile views. |
| **Certificate Viewing** | **View 5 certificates** per unique profile per day. (Encourages subscription for deep dive). | **Unlimited certificate viewing** across all profiles. | Unlimited certificate viewing. |
| **Advanced Search** | No advanced filtering. | **Filter Profiles by Skills, Certifications, and Issuing Body.** | All Recruiter features. |
| **Commenting/Rating** | Standard commenting, no trust score rating. | Ability to leave **Private Notes** on profiles and assign **Internal Trust Scores.** | Private Notes + Integration with **Internal HR/ATS System.** |
| **Bulk Actions** | None. | **Save and Organize Profiles** into folders (e.g., "Shortlist"). | **Bulk Data Retrieval** via secure API (for approved use cases). |

---

### 🔑 Key Business Logic Principles

1.  **Strict Upload Limit Enforcement:** The core of your monetization for uploaders is the limit. Once a user hits the **Free Tier limit of 5**, the system must block further uploads until they either **delete an existing certificate** or **upgrade to a paid subscription**.
2.  **Viewer Rate Limiting:** The viewing limit (e.g., "View 5 certificates per profile per day") acts as a powerful barrier. It allows guests to check a profile's quality but forces power users (like recruiters) to subscribe for efficiency. The logic should reset the counter every 24 hours per unique IP/session.
3.  **Tiered Verification:** Make verification a high-value feature. The manual, human-reviewed verification should only be available to paid tiers. This validates the subscription cost and boosts the credibility of your platform.
